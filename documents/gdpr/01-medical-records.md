# Subject access request — medical records

**Send to:** your GP practice. Address it to *the Practice Manager or Data Protection
Officer*. Many practices have an online form or a dedicated email — search
*"[practice name] subject access request"* first and use that if it exists.

**Covers:** the record your GP practice holds. Hospital records are held separately by
each NHS trust, so if you want those, send a copy of this letter to each trust's Data
Protection Officer with the wording in the note at the bottom.

## Notes before you send

**The "serious harm" exemption is the one that applies here**, and it is narrow: a
clinician may withhold information likely to cause serious harm to your physical or
mental health. It is not a general discretion, and if it is used they should say so.

**Third-party information may be redacted** — where the record names another person and
it would not be reasonable to disclose their details.

**For hospital records**, replace point 1 with:

> A complete copy of the records held by the Trust relating to my care, including
> inpatient and outpatient notes, correspondence, discharge summaries, test and
> imaging results, for the period [DATE RANGE, or "all records held"].

**A date range is optional and can help.** Asking for everything is your right, but
narrowing to a period you actually care about often produces a faster and more useful
response. If you do not know what period matters, ask for everything.
