# CBT or a car licence — the costs laid out

Backlog **#46**, whose rationale set the split: *"Cost, steps and timeline I can lay out.
Booking is yours."* Prepared 19 August 2026.

**Read the ordering section first.** It changes what the decision is.

---

## 1. Neither route starts until the provisional licence is replaced

Goal step 2 is *"confirm the provisional licence is in hand and current"*, and that is
**backlog #48 — overdue in your diary since 6 August, now 13 days.**

You cannot take CBT without a provisional licence, and you cannot book a car theory test
without one either. So this is not a step that sits alongside the decision; it is the gate
in front of both, and it is the cheapest thing on this page:

> **Replacing a lost, stolen, defaced or destroyed licence: £20.** Same price online or
> by post.

The goal's own next step is *"establish what actually happened to it — lost, stolen,
damaged, or expired"*, because the answer changes the form. **That is a five-minute
question and it is holding up a decision you have been carrying for weeks.**

## 2. The government fees are almost identical, and that is the finding

This is the part worth knowing before comparing anything else. All figures from GOV.UK,
fetched 19 August 2026.

| | Motorcycle route | Car route |
|---|---|---|
| Replace provisional | £20 | £20 |
| Theory test | £23 | £23 |
| Practical — module 1 (off-road) | £15.50 | — |
| Practical — on-road, weekday | £75 | £62 |
| **Fixed DVLA/DVSA fees** | **£133.50** | **£105.00** |

Evening and weekend practical tests cost more: **£88.50** motorcycle, **£75** car.

**£28.50 separates the two routes on official fees.** That is not a decision. The entire
real difference is in the part the government does not price — **training** — and that is
precisely what goal step 3 asks you to find out by ringing two schools.

---

## 3. What the state does not price, and I will not invent

**CBT has no published price.** GOV.UK's own CBT page states no cost, because it is set by
each training school. I am not going to put a number here that I cannot source — a
plausible figure you then budget against is worse than a blank.

**Driving lessons have no published price either**, and the total depends on how many
hours you need, which nobody can tell you in advance.

**What that means practically:** two phone calls settle this decision, and no amount of
desk research substitutes for them. Ask each school for the CBT day rate, and each
instructor for their hourly rate and how many hours they would expect. Write the actual
quotes into goal step 3 rather than an estimate.

---

## 4. What CBT actually buys you

Sourced from GOV.UK's CBT page:

- You may ride a **moped at 16+**, or a **motorcycle up to 125cc and 11kW at 17+**.
- **L plates are required.**
- The certificate lasts **two years**. Within that time you must pass the full test, or
  **retake CBT or stop riding**.

**Not confirmed, and worth asking the school:** whether you may carry a pillion passenger
and whether motorways are permitted on a provisional entitlement. Both are standard
restrictions as I understand them, but I could not confirm them on GOV.UK in two attempts,
so treat them as questions rather than facts.

**The shape of the difference**, then, is that CBT is *one day and you are mobile, with
limits and a two-year clock*, while a car licence is *many weeks and you are not mobile
until the end, with no limits afterwards*.

## 5. Against your actual money

This is the part no brochure can tell you, and it is the reason the routes are not equally
available today.

| | |
|---|---|
| Budget headroom, August | **£191.52** — and the budget does not yet cover every category, so this is a ceiling, not a balance |
| Last known bank balance | **£0.03**, as of 11 August |
| Spending guard | £20 per transaction |

**The £20 licence replacement fits inside anything.** It is affordable today on any reading.

**A CBT day is plausibly within one month's headroom** once you have a quote — but the
headroom figure is a ceiling over an incomplete budget, so treat it as "possibly" and not
"yes".

**A full car licence is not affordable from current headroom**, and this is arithmetic
rather than an opinion: the fees are only £105, but the lessons are the cost, and they run
to many multiples of a month's headroom on any realistic hourly rate. That does not rule it
out — it makes it a saving plan with a date, not a booking.

## 6. So the decision in front of you is smaller than it looks

1. **Replace the provisional. £20. It gates both routes and it is 13 days overdue.**
2. **Ring two CBT schools and one driving instructor.** Write the real quotes into goal
   step 3. That is the only missing number in this entire document.
3. **Then choose** — and by then it will mostly have chosen itself, because one of the two
   will be affordable this month and the other will be a plan for next year.
